This Trademark Policy ("Policy") describes how the names, logos, and other
brand indicators of the Dixlase project, operated by exc-D inc. ("exc-D"),
may and may not be used by third parties. It supplements — but does not
replace — applicable trademark and unfair-competition law.

## 2. Goals of This Policy

The goals of this Policy are:

1. To allow honest, accurate references to the Dixlase project, including
   in documentation, blog posts, books, conference talks, and educational
   material.
2. To allow third-party plugins, themes, and integrations to clearly
   describe their compatibility with Dixlase.
3. To prevent uses that confuse end users about the **origin** of software
   or services — in particular, uses that imply official endorsement,
   affiliation, or sponsorship when none exists.
4. To preserve exc-D's ability to maintain the integrity of the Dixlase
   brand as the project matures into a commercial marketplace (Phase 2+).

This Policy is **not** intended to prevent free discussion of Dixlase, to
restrict legitimate interoperability, or to override rights granted by the
AGPL license and the Plugin and Theme Exception described in
[`COPYRIGHT-POLICY.md`](./COPYRIGHT-POLICY.md). The copyright license and
the trademark policy are independent: receiving the software under the
AGPL does **not** grant any trademark license.

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Prior Permission Required)

You may use the Dixlase Marks **without prior permission from exc-D** in
the following circumstances, provided the use is truthful and not
misleading:

### 3.1 Nominative / Referential Use

Referring to Dixlase by name in order to identify the project, for
example:

- "Plugin for Dixlase"
- "Compatible with Dixlase v0.1"
- "Tutorial: Installing Dixlase on Ubuntu"
- "Migrating from WordPress to Dixlase"

### 3.2 Truthful Descriptive Use for Plugins, Themes, and Integrations

You may use "**for Dixlase**" or "**Dixlase-compatible**" as a descriptive
suffix on the name of your plugin, theme, hosting product, or integration.

| ✅ Permitted | ❌ Not Permitted |
|---|---|
| "Acme Forms **for Dixlase**" | "Dixlase Forms" (suggests an official plugin) |
| "Acme **Dixlase-compatible** Hosting" | "Dixlase Hosting" (suggests an official service) |
| "Acme Importer **for Dixlase**" | "Official Dixlase Importer" |
| "Compatible with Dixlase" badge on your site | A logo lock-up that combines your logo with the Dixlase logo |

The general rule: **your product or service name must not begin with
"Dixlase"** unless you have written permission from exc-D. The Dixlase
name may follow your distinctive name, separated by "for", "by", or
"compatible with".

### 3.3 Use in Discussion, Reviews, Education, and News

Books, articles, blog posts, video tutorials, conference talks,
benchmarks, and reviews may freely reference Dixlase by name and quote
short factual descriptions. Educational use is encouraged.

### 3.4 Use in Source Code Comments and Documentation

Source code and documentation that interoperates with Dixlase may
reference the project name in comments, docblocks, README files, and
manifest fields (e.g. `"package_name": "yourvendor/yourplugin"`,
`"requires": {"dixlase": "^0.1"}`).

## 4. Uses That Require Written Permission

The following uses require **written permission** from exc-D in advance
(email info@dixlase.org):

1. Using the Dixlase **logo** in any commercial product, packaging,
   merchandise, or paid service offering.
2. Using "Dixlase" as the first or dominant element of a company name,
   product name, domain name, or service name (e.g. "Dixlase Pro",
   "Dixlase Cloud", "Dixlase Hosting", "Dixlase Marketplace",
   "dixlase-hosting.com").
3. Distributing modified versions of Dixlase under the Dixlase name (see
   Section 5).
4. Operating a marketplace, app store, or plugin directory under a name
   that contains "Dixlase".
5. Operating a SaaS or managed hosting service under a name that
   contains "Dixlase".
6. Producing merchandise (stickers, T-shirts, signage) featuring the
   Dixlase logo for sale or large-scale free distribution.
7. Indicating in any marketing material that your product, company, or
   service is "**official**", "**authorized**", "**certified**", or
   "**endorsed**" by Dixlase or exc-D when no such relationship exists.

Where permission is granted, it will typically be in the form of a
written authorization specifying the scope and duration of permitted
use. exc-D may revoke or modify such authorization on reasonable notice
if use becomes misleading or inconsistent with the goals in Section 2.

## 5. Modified Versions and Forks

The AGPL license grants the right to modify and redistribute the Dixlase
source code. The trademark policy adds the following requirement:

**If you distribute a modified version of Dixlase, you must rename it.**

The rename must be sufficiently distinct that end users cannot confuse
your fork with the official Dixlase project. Acceptable forms include:

- A wholly different name (e.g. "Acme CMS, a Dixlase fork")
- A name that places your distinctive mark first (e.g. "Acme Dixlase
  Edition") — **only with written permission**

You **may** state factually that your fork is "based on Dixlase" or
"derived from Dixlase". You **may not** continue to call your fork
"Dixlase", "Dixlase Community Edition", "Dixlase Free", or similar.

This requirement is consistent with the Mozilla Firefox / Iceweasel and
Red Hat Enterprise Linux / Rocky Linux precedents.

## 6. Plugin and Theme Authors

If you publish a plugin or theme that interoperates with Dixlase
(including for sale through the future Dixlase marketplace), the
following guidance applies:

1. **Naming.** Follow Section 3.2: your plugin/theme name must not begin
   with "Dixlase". Use forms like "Acme Forms for Dixlase".
2. **Manifest fields.** The `plugin.json` / `theme.json` fields
   (`package_name`, `slug`, `provides`, `requires`, etc.) may reference
   "dixlase" freely — these are technical identifiers, not branding.
3. **Author identity fields.** Use your own `author_id`. Reserved
   `author_id` values such as `dixlase`, `exc-d-inc`, and
   `dixlase-authority-*` may not be used by third parties.
4. **Logo.** Do not embed the Dixlase logo in your plugin/theme branding
   or in icons used for distribution.
5. **Marketplace submission.** When the official Dixlase marketplace
   opens, additional naming guidance may apply at submission time;
   submission terms will be published separately.

exc-D will investigate reports but does not commit to any specific
enforcement timeline. Trademark enforcement is at exc-D's discretion.
